Reuse [EXPATS safeguards](EXPAT_PRIVACY_SAFEGUARD_MODEL.md), [global boundary](../contracts/GLOBAL_MARKET_COMMERCIAL_BOUNDARY.md), [cross-tenant isolation](../contracts/CROSS_TENANT_ISOLATION_MODEL.md) and [legal basis](../contracts/CONSENT_LEGAL_BASIS_MODEL.md).

## Data and access planes

Public catalogue/rules/source metadata are separate from private originals, extracted personal facts and organization cases. Check authenticated actor, subject, purpose, legal basis/authority, capability, interval and recipient scope before loading. Do not infer entitlement from nationality, locale, payer, creator or organization membership alone. Place access-controlled evidence references in events, not raw documents, secrets, health/protection details or payment data.

Explicitly separate individual, family, health, professional and organization views. Equivalent verified parental authority must yield equal capabilities regardless of mother/father or payer. Adult partner access requires its own grant. Child handover re-evaluates access while preserving lawful history. Organization departure does not confiscate personal originals. A specialist receives only chosen relevant evidence/chronology and scope, not blanket access or implicit legal representation.

## Sharing

ShareGrant references subject, claim/document IDs, purpose, permitted recipient or justified bearer scope, expiry, use/revocation policy and audit. QR/URL is transport, not access policy. No private indexation, public object URLs, analytics copies or referrer leakage. Robots/noindex alone is insufficient security. An invalid/expired/revoked link must not leak existence through excessive errors. Cache freshness/revocation must be checked at the actual reliance boundary. Generic previews and safe-channel preferences apply to sensitive cases.

## Content and input safety

Malware/quarantine and untrusted-content controls apply before extraction. Document text cannot instruct tools to reveal another case, access credentials or publish a rule. Do not normalize identity aliases by similarity alone. Preserve original script/date/calendar and confirmation provenance. Pack members have size/type/path limits, safe decompression and integrity checks; no arbitrary executable content. Public content pipelines must never copy user originals to seed datasets, logs or repository fixtures.

## Residency and market operations

Before a new served market decide operator obligations, supported service class, storage/processing locations, transfer basis, subprocessor roles and retention. The architecture makes no blanket national legal assurance. Locale/document intake support is not permission to sell there. Global origin, target activity and operational deployment region are independent. Stronger boundaries for minors, protection cases and health remain mandatory; no automatic home-state embassy referral in unsafe protection circumstances.

## Reliability and privacy lifecycle

Use encryption/key management through approved ports, least-privilege credentials, backup/restore drills, incident process, monitoring and scoped diagnostics. Retention and deletion operate by lawful purpose, record type, hold and revocation policy; an audit trail is not authorization to retain all originals forever. Replay metadata must be minimized yet explain historical decisions. Restores must not silently revive revoked access or republish withdrawn rules. Offline/pending operations reconcile against server revisions; no last-write-wins for regulated truth.

## Pilot stop conditions

Missing authentication or scoped loading, untested recovery, unresolved critical privacy defect, unavailable required provider, stale critical rule/translation, unreviewed market legality or unsupported core workflow blocks real-person paid use. Demonstrate with synthetic data instead. No response may infer a medical, immigration, employment or political judgment beyond supported data and purpose. Human review, correction channels, transparent limitations and user agency are required. These are architecture requirements; this change performs no penetration test, medical/legal review or runtime deployment.
