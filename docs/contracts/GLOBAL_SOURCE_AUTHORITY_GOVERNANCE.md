## SourceSnapshot

Required: stable source ID, publisher/authority reference, jurisdiction and subject/activity scope, source type, original language, URL or acquisition reference, content digest/version, publication/effective/retrieved timestamps where known, review status, reviewer competence/scope, reviewed_at, next_review policy, access/use license, permitted collection method, restrictions, supersession/conflict references and attribution. Absence is explicit UNKNOWN. Fetch time is not legal verification time; an official-looking domain or logo does not alone verify authority or all row claims.

Kinds distinguish legislation, official guidance, public register, individual decision, form/template index, professional-body content, educational record, industry/private certification, user claim and community report. Their evidentiary effect differs by use case. A directory can support discovery without supporting a deadline, fee or positive entitlement.

## AuthorityMandate

Keep stable authority identity separate from time-bound mandate: credential families, issue/verify/recognize functions, jurisdictions, subject types, effective interval, predecessor/successor, source and review. An institution rename is not a new mandate; succession does not automatically transfer every historical function. Current registry trust is not proof of historical issuing power.

## Publishing process

Discovery → check access/use rights → preserve snapshot → extraction proposal → qualified substantive review → language review → scenario verification → controlled publication → impact monitoring. Editors cannot self-approve critical publication; role identity, competence and purpose are auditable. AI may draft/classify/compare/summarize but not publish authority facts by itself. Contributors can report missing items; contribution scores and sponsor money cannot determine legal truth.

## Source-specific boundaries preserved from the proposal

PRADO supports selected document categories/templates, not complete licence counts or holder verification. ESCO supports semantic occupation/skill alignment, not profession permission. Diia, DigiLocker, ABLIS, UK registers and other portal names are candidate source/adapter directions; existence is not API access or data-reuse authorization. W3C/OpenID/EDC/EUDI references require a separately reviewed implementation/version matrix before integration; cryptography does not determine applicability.

## Change and incident response

A source change is a candidate event until reviewed. Record source→rule→definition→path→recognition→current case→translation→notice dependencies. Withdraw a defective publication, quarantine conflicting records, locate affected assessments, issue a controlled correction and preserve immutable before/after lineage. No silent retroactive rewrite. Review freshness is based on risk and actual source change cadence, not one universal interval. Source/provider outage explicitly downgrades assurance; no fabricated fresh verification.

## Operational ownership

Assign content operations owner plus qualified jurisdiction/sector and linguistic reviewers. Estimate and measure ongoing update workload separately from initial inventory count. Before a market pilot verify content access rights, reviewer capacity, incident escalation and data processing responsibilities. Content governance is a production prerequisite, not an implied service of an LLM or a marketplace provider.
