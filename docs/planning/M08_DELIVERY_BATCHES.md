# CALPQ M08 Delivery Batches

Status: `PLANNING ONLY / IMPLEMENTATION BLOCKED`
ID: `CALPQ-M08-DELIVERY-0001`

## Batch M08-A — Organization, Membership & Delegation
Scope:
1. organization profile;
2. membership model;
3. role model;
4. delegation scope and validity;
5. tenant/organization binding;
6. audit/access integration.

Definition of Done:
- organization/membership/role/delegation are explicit distinct concepts;
- delegation is scoped, time-bounded where applicable and attributable;
- role or delegation does not transfer professional competence;
- tenant isolation and access-purpose rules remain enforced.

## Batch M08-B — Assignment Requirement Profiles & Guard
Scope:
1. AssignmentRequirementProfile;
2. person/activity/resource context;
3. credential/eligibility/condition inputs;
4. assignment outcomes;
5. review/override boundary;
6. decision evidence.

Definition of Done:
- outcomes are governed and machine-readable;
- assignability never derives solely from job title or role;
- conditions and unresolved states remain explicit;
- review/override decisions are attributable and evidence-backed.

## Batch M08-C — Organization Compliance Projection & Export
Scope:
1. organization compliance projection;
2. workforce/assignment status aggregation;
3. expiring/missing credential visibility;
4. compliance export;
5. audit/provenance linkage;
6. action queue integration.

Definition of Done:
- projection is not authoritative source of individual credential truth;
- exported compliance state is versioned/explainable;
- minimum necessary disclosure applies;
- organization next actions derive from governed individual/assignment states.

## Exit
M08 exit requires organization/delegation, assignment guard and compliance projection/export with proof that organizational authority does not fabricate professional competence.
